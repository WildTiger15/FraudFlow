FraudFlow

An AI assisted fraud and anti money laundering alert review prototype. Rules and anomaly detection flag activity, an AI agent drafts the case, automatic checks verify every fact, and a person approves, edits or rejects every case.

Synthetic PaySim data only: no real customers. Read-only public demo.

How it works

  1. 1. Flag6 explainable rules plus an Isolation Forest screen 454,386 transfers and cash outs.
  2. 2. GatherFor each alert, collect the flagged transaction and both accounts' recent activity.
  3. 3. DraftClaude writes a case summary and UTR-style report fields, citing transaction IDs.
  4. 4. CheckEvery number and ID in the draft is matched to the data; failures are marked low confidence.
  5. 5. DecideA named reviewer approves, edits or rejects; the decision and time are logged.
  6. 6. MeasureDetection quality, decisions and review time on a dashboard.

Detection quality

Measured per transaction against PaySim's fraud labels on simulated days 1 to 3. Thresholds were revised once for alert volume with labels visible, so these are optimistic (see the detection notes on GitHub).
